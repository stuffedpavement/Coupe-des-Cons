The Rules
Simple enough for a Con

The Format

12 players. 48 World Cup teams. Each player is randomly assigned 4 teams — one from each strength tier — giving everyone a mix of favourites, contenders, and underdogs.

The Tiers

Teams are split into 4 tiers of 12 based on the official FIFA/Coca-Cola Men's World Rankings, April 1st 2026. Each player receives one team from each tier.

The Three Prizes

🥇
Overall Winner — £30

Whoever owns the team that wins the World Cup on 19 July. Simple as that.

📈
Most Improved — £15

Whoever owns the team that climbs the most FIFA ranking places between April 1st 2026 and the first rankings update after the tournament. A surprise run deep into the knockouts could swing this.

🥄
Wooden Spoon — £15

Whoever's four teams accumulate the fewest points across the entire tournament. Points: Win = 3, Draw = 1, Loss = 0. Tiebreaker: fewest goals scored.

The Prize Pot

£5 entry × 12 players = £60 total. Overall winner takes £30, Most Improved takes £15, Wooden Spoon takes £15.

The Draw

Teams are assigned by a verifiably random draw using a public random seed, locked at a fixed timestamp before any results are shown. One tier is revealed per day, starting with Tier 4 on Friday 5 June and finishing with Tier 1 on Monday 8 June — two days before the tournament kicks off.
